On September 9, 2019, the school district filed a Request for Withdrawal, stating 
that the Board of Education of the school district had voted to rescind its dismissal 
action against respondent  Edward Carter (respondent)  and that the school district thus 
was requesting dismissal of the above -captioned matter, without prejudice. 
Respondent  then requested, pursuant to Education Code section 44944, subdivision 
(d)(1)(C), and the holding in Boliou v. St ockton Unified School District (2012) 207 
Cal.App. 4th 170, 177-179, that the Office of Administrative Hearings issue a written 
determination that respondent should not be dismissed.   
 2 On September 18, 2019, a telephonic status conference was held before 
Presiding Administrative Law Judge Susan L. Formaker (PALJ Formaker) to discuss the 
issues raised by the parties’ papers on the request for dismissal. Later on September 
18, 2019, the school district  filed the parties’ joint stipulation  (“joint stipulation”), in 
accordance with the discussion during the telephonic status conference,  regarding the 
school district’s withdrawal of the dismissal action . The joint stipulation is the basis for 
the following findings, conclusions, and order:  The record was closed and the matter 
was submitted for decision before PALJ Formaker on September 18, 2019.  
FACTUAL FINDINGS  
1. Respondent is a permanent certified employee of the school district.  
2. On April 5, 2019, the school district issued a Statement of Charges 
seeking respondent’s  immediate suspension without pay and dismissal for alleged 
causes specified in Education Code sections 44932 and 44939. Respondent submitted a Request for Hearing and Notice of Defense to the Statement of Charges  on June 7, 
2019 . On June 12, 2019, the sch ool district filed and served its Accusation against 
respondent. On September 3, 2019, the Board of Education of the school district voted 
to rescind its dismissal action against respondent. The school district has requested 
the withdrawal and dismissal of  this matter,  without prejudice.  
3. Pursuant to the parties’ joint stipulation,  the parties have waive d the right 
to convene a Commission on Professional Competence  and have stipulated to having 
the hearing conducted by a single administrative law judge pursuant to Education 
Code section 44944, subdivision (c)(1) . The parties’ joint stipulation further provides 
that the administrative law judge conducting the hearing shall have all the powers  
 3 provided under Education Code section 44944, subdivision (c)(1) , shall decide the 
matter , and shall prepare a written decision pursuant to Education Code section 
44944, subdivision (d)(1) . Through the joint stipulation, the parties agree and request 
that the decision contain  a disposition that respondent should not  be dismissed.  
4. A formal hearing is not required, and would constitute an undue burden 
on the parties, given the parties’ joint stipulation. This Decision is issued based on  the 
parties’ joint stipulation , in lieu of a formal hearing . 
LEGAL CONCLUSIONS  
1. Base d on the school district’s rescission of the dismissal action against 
respondent  and the parties’ joint stipulation, cause does not exist for respondent’s 
dismissal under either Education Code section 44932 or Education Code section 44939.  
2. Based on the joint stipulation  and the ruling in  Boliou v. Stockton Unified 
School District (2012) 207 Cal.App.4th 170,177- 179, respondent is entitled to a 
disposition that he should not be dismissed.  
   
 4 ORDER  
The Statement of Charges and Accusation are dismissed. Respondent should 
not be di smissed  from his employment with the Los Angeles Unified School District .
