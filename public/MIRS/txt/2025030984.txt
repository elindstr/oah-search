represented responding party, Los Angeles Unified School District (District). 2 PROCEDURAL HISTORY
On February 19, 2025, the District issued a statement of charges against Mr. 
Fritch alleging he engaged in immoral conduct with respect to his 12th grade student 
Sadia O., pursuant to Education Code section 44932, subdivision (a)(1). The statement 
of charges seeks Mr. Fritch’s dismissal and his immediate suspension without pay 
pursuant to Education Code sections 44932 and 44939. The statement of charges and 
notice of immediate suspension were served upon Mr. Fritch on February 19, 2025. 
Based upon the charge of immoral conduct, Mr. Fritch is on immediate suspension 
without pay pursuant to Education Code section 44939.
On March 25, 2025, Mr. Fritch filed this motion for immediate reversal of his 
suspension without pay, pursuant to Education Code section 44939, subdivision (c)(1). 
Mr. Fritch argues that the District has not alleged sufficient facts to constitute immoral 
conduct to place him on immediate unpaid status. Mr. Fritch asks for an order 
reversing his suspension without pay. DISCUSSION 
Education Code section 44939, subdivision (b), provides, in part, that a school 
district may immediately suspend a permanent employee who has been charged “with 
immoral conduct, conviction of a felony or of any crime involving moral turpitude, with 
incompetency due to mental disability, with willful refusal to perform regular 
assignments without reasonable cause, as prescribed by reasonable rules and 
regulations of the employing school district, or with violation of (Education Code) 
[s]ection 51530.”
