FACTUAL FINDINGS 
1. At its meeting on December 11, 2024, the Governing Board (Board) of 
the District adopted District Resolution 12: 2024-2025. This resolution declares the 2 intention at the end of the 2024 2025 academic year to reduce certificated 
employee services as stated in the resolution, with the total amount of reduction 
equivalent to 23.7686 full time employees (FTE). 
2. At its meeting on February 26, 2025, the District Board adopted District 
Resolution 17: 2024-2025. This resolution declares  intention at the end of 
the 2024 2025 academic year to reduce certificated employee services as stated in the 
resolution, with the total amount of reduction equivalent to 10.2418 FTE. The 
reductions Resolution 17: 2024-2025 describes are in addition to the reductions 
Resolution 12: 2024-2025 describes. 
3. Acting in her official capacity as  Director of Human 
Resources, complainant Patricia Perez gave timely notice in writing to 19 certificated 
District employees that the District Board proposed to release them from employment 
in accordance with Resolutions 12: 2024-2025 and 17: 2024-2025. 
4. Two of these employees requested hearings and, after service to them of 
District Statements of Reduction in Force, returned notices of participation in the 
hearing. Subsequently, one employee withdrew her hearing request. The hearing 
proceeded with respect only to respondent Sara Rosenbloom. 
5. Complainant alleges that the District must release respondent 
Rosenbloom from employment because the District has chosen to reduce or eliminate 
the service she provides, and she is the only employee who provides this service. 
6. Respondent Rosenbloom holds a clear single-subject credential 
authorizing her to teach theater. The District employed her during the 2024 2025 
academic year as its only drama teacher. She teaches about half time (hours equivalent 3 to 0.506 FTE) at Kennedy Middle School, and also advises an extracurricular drama 
club. 
7. During the 2024 2025 academic year, the District covered the cost of 
local funds it had raised from a parcel tax (Measure U). 
The District spends Measure U funds that are available for use at Kennedy Middle 
School in accordance with guidance from a School Site Council (SSC) at the school. 
8. At its meeting in January 2025, the Kennedy Middle School SSC discussed 
the likelihood that Measure U funding for the 2025 2026 academic year would be 
inadequate to pay for both a part-time drama teacher and a full-time cross-curricular 
Science, Technology, Engineering, Arts, and Mathematics (STEAM) teacher. The 
majority of the SSC preferred, in that event, to retain the STEAM teacher and forego a 
drama teacher. 
9. Resolution 17: 2024-2025 states that the District will discontinue 
after the 2024 2025 
academic year. This reduction will leave the District with no drama teacher, and will 
eliminate the only position for which respondent is certificated and competent. 
10. The Parent-Teacher Organization (PTO) at Kennedy Middle School has 
pledged $60,000 to the District for the 2025 2026 academic year, on the condition 
that the District use these funds to recreate a part-
Kennedy Middle School. The District has accepted similar funding in the past. To 
accept and use these funds for the 2025 2026 academic year, the District must confirm 
the amount of time such a drama teacher would work, as well as the required 
credential(s) and any other special working conditions; must communicate this plan to 4 District provide the teacher to the school. These steps have not yet occurred. If they 
do, the District would consider this PTO-funded position one into which respondent 
has the first right to be rehired. 
11. During the 2024 2025 academic year, respondent developed several 
disagreements with District administrators and with the Kennedy Middle School 
principal over her compensation, and over her need for time and privacy to store 
breast milk for her infant. Respondent and the District have resolved some, but not all, 
of these disputes. No evidence shows that anyone on the District Board was aware of 
these disputes when the Board determined through Resolution 17: 2024-2025 that the 
District should eliminate its drama teacher after the 2024 2025 academic year. 
12. This decision addresses all challenges respondent Rosenbloom made to 
the 2024 2025 academic year. Any contentions not specifically addressed in this 
decision are found to be without merit and are rejected. To the extent that other 
challenges may have been possible but were not identified during testimony or in oral 
or written argument, respondent has waived those challenges.
