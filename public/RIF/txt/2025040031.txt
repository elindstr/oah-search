1. At its meeting on February 20, 2025, the Governing Board (Board) of the 
Benicia Unified School District (District) adopted District Resolution 24-25-25. This 
resolution declares  intention at the end of the 2024 2025 academic year 
to reduce certificated employee services as stated in the resolution, with the total 
amount of reduction equivalent to six full-time employees. 
2. Acting in her official capacity as  Assistant Superintendent 
for Human Resources, Pat Jennings, Ed.D., gave timely notice in writing to four District 
employees that the District Board proposed to release them from employment in 
accordance with Resolution 24-25-25. 
3. Two of the employees to whom Jennings directed the notices described 
in Finding 2, Julie Cohen and Navneet Hayer, timely requested hearings on their 
re-employment.

recommends that the District Board release the respondents named in Finding 3 from 
employment, after the 2024 2025 academic year, in the positions in which they have 
served during this academic year. Complainant alleges that the District should release 
these employees because it has chosen to reduce or eliminate services that they 
provide, or because other persons with greater seniority will displace them from their 
positions.

multiple subject credentials would have been equal in seniority to respondents, rather 
than senior to respondents, and Jennings would have needed to apply the tie-breaking 
criteria to choose only two of these four persons for release from employment. 
16. The District does not propose to retain any other elementary school 
teacher with a multiple subject credential whose first date of service to the District was 
on or after August 7, 2024.
