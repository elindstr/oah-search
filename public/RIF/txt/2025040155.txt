FACTUAL FINDINGS 
1. On February 24, 2025, the San Mateo County Superintendent of Schools 
adopted Resolution No. 25-01, reducing or discontinuing particular kinds of services 
for the 2025-2026 school year, and directing the Superintendent or her designee to 
send appropriate notices to all employees whose positions may be affected by the 2 reduction or discontinuation of services. Resolution 25-01 calls for the reduction of 
1 FTE (full-time equivalent) certificated employee. 
2. Resolution 25-01 calls for the elimination of the following position: Nurse 
on Special Assignment (NOSA). 
3. The services set forth in Resolution No. 25-01 are particular kinds of 
services that may be reduced or discontinued within the meaning of Education Code 
section 44955. Associate Superintendent of Human Resources Tami Moore testified 
that the reduction of services is necessary because the services provided by the NOSA 
are no longer needed. The position was created during the COVID-19 pandemic to 
develop and implement health policies in response to the public health crisis. 
4. Pursuant to Education Code section 44955, subdivision (b), if a county 
office of education reduces particular kinds of services resulting in a decrease in the 
number of certificated, permanent employees, the services of a permanent employee 
may not be terminated if another employee with less seniority is retained to render a

10. Respondent testified about the various important public health projects 
she has worked on since starting her position in 2021. Although she initially worked on 
COVID-19 related matters, over time her work involved other health and safety issues. 
She described her position as a nurse leadership role, with oversight over the 50 
nurses in the various school districts in the county. She has provided training to nurses 
and supported nurses in furthering their education. Respondent also has worked with 
the California Department of Public Health and San Mateo County public health 
officials to improve immunization rates, supply school sites with Narcan for treating 
narcotics overdoses, develop policies regarding exposure notifications, and prepare for 
an anticipated measles outbreak. 
11. Respondent believes it is not in the best interest of the more than 90,000 
students in the county for SMCOE to eliminate her position. She noted that studies 
have shown the numerous benefits of school nurses. Respondent also believes that 
SMCOE is planning to create a new nurse leadership position to perform the duties 
she has been performing but requiring an administrator credential.

requirements set forth in Education Code sections 44949 and 44955. (Factual Findings 
5 and 6.) 
2. The services referenced in the Resolution set forth in Factual Finding 1 
are the kind that may be reduced or discontinued in accordance with applicable 
statutes and case law. (See Ed. Code, § 44955; 
 (1978) 76 Cal.App.3d 796; (1976) 64 Cal.App.3d 
167.) The  decision to reduce or discontinue the services is 
neither arbitrary nor capricious, but rather a proper exercise of her discretion.
